Twenty minutes undo an afternoon

Aungier makes both, and a side we had already beaten twice takes the last game of the season.

Saturday 14 March 2026 · Big Side

Man of the match Callum Aungier Two assists · his eighth appearance of the season
The goals Bonham-Lloyd, Simeon Both made by Aungier

Two up and comfortable at the break, and then twenty minutes that undid the whole afternoon.

Callum Aungier made both and did not deserve the scoreline he got for it. Dom Bonham-Lloyd finished the first from six yards; Tom Simeon took the second early enough that a third looked likelier than a comeback.

Stoics got their equaliser from a corner we had already survived twice, and the winner from the same corner five minutes later. It ends a season in which we beat them 5–1 and 4–1 and then lost the one they will remember.
