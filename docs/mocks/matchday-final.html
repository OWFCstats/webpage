Match report

Two up and comfortable at the break, and then twenty minutes that undid the whole afternoon. Callum Aungier made both goals and did not deserve the scoreline he got for it — Dom Bonham-Lloyd finished the first from six yards, and Tom Simeon took the second early enough that a third looked likelier than a comeback.

Stoics got their equaliser from a corner we had already survived twice, and the winner from the same corner five minutes later. Eight of us on a cold Saturday against a side we had beaten 5–1 and 4–1 already this season, and the legs went at exactly the wrong time.

It ends 2025/26 with a loss, which flatters neither the afternoon nor the season — though there is something in finishing a year having taken twelve points off a division that put seven past us in September. Cheltonians on the 28th, and then a summer to think about it.
